Our Farm

Ko-Matha Dairy Farm follows hygienic and sustainable dairy farming practices. The farm works with Indian A1 cow holders to ensure quality milk production.

Modern technology is used to monitor animal health, milk quality, storage conditions and distribution.

Our goal is to provide customers with transparent, fresh and nutritious dairy products.
