Masala Dosa

🍽️ Ingredients

2 cups rice
1 cup urad dal
3 potatoes
Onion and spices

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Soak rice and urad dal.
  2. Grind them into a smooth batter.
  3. Ferment the batter overnight.
  4. Prepare potato masala.
  5. Spread batter on a hot pan.
  6. Add potato masala and serve hot.

Serving Size

Ingredients shown for 2 people.
